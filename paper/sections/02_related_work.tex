\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Neural rendering and Gaussian scene representations.}
Neural radiance fields synthesize novel views by optimizing a continuous
radiance field from posed images~\cite{mildenhall2020nerf}.  3D Gaussian
Splatting replaces repeated volumetric sampling with an explicit set of
anisotropic Gaussians and a differentiable rasterizer, enabling high-quality
real-time rendering~\cite{kerbl20233dgs}.  Our work does not modify the 3DGS
representation; it studies which observations should be acquired under a fixed
training budget.

\paragraph{Active view selection.}
Next-best-view and active reconstruction methods balance information gain,
coverage, visibility, and motion cost.  Learned systems may predict rendering
error or geometric uncertainty, while classical controls favor unexplored
space.  We isolate the marginal value of model-derived uncertainty by matching
all policies to the same seed, holdout, and target budget and by including both
acquisition-time and camera-pose coverage controls.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and selective prediction.}
Uncertainty is useful only insofar as it predicts error or improves a downstream
decision.  We therefore report rank correlation, failure-detection AUROC and
AUPRC, risk--coverage behavior, and sparsification error in addition to the
acquisition result.  We do not interpret the renderer signals as calibrated
probabilities.

\paragraph{RGB-D reconstruction benchmarks.}
ICL-NUIM provides synthetic RGB-D sequences with published camera trajectories
and surface ground truth for controlled reconstruction evaluation
~\cite{handa2014benchmark}.  We use its four living-room trajectories as
independent acquisition environments and preserve their metric coordinate
system during training and evaluation.
