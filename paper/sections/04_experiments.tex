\section{Experiments}

\paragraph{Data and splits.}
We uniformly sample 240 observations, including both endpoints, from each of
the four clean ICL-NUIM living-room trajectories.  Each trajectory has 25 seed
training frames, 10 validation frames, and a contiguous 20-frame test block.
A five-sampled-frame guard band excludes nearby candidates on either side of
each holdout where the sequence boundary permits.  Training, validation, and
test filenames are disjoint.

\paragraph{Training.}
All models use Nerfstudio Splatfacto~\cite{tancik2023nerfstudio} for 7,000
iterations on NVIDIA L4 GPUs.
Camera optimization is disabled.  We disable orientation, centering, and pose
auto-scaling so rendered depth and fused surfaces remain in the published
metric frame.  Primary active--random comparisons use optimization seeds 42
and 43 on every trajectory; coverage controls use seed 42.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
RGB metrics are PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS.  Depth metrics include raw and per-frame
median-aligned AbsRel and threshold accuracy.  For surface evaluation, predicted
and reference depth from all held-out frames are backprojected through the
published intrinsics and poses, capped deterministically, and voxelized at
\SI{2}{cm}.  We report bidirectional nearest-neighbor accuracy and completeness,
their symmetric mean (Chamfer-L1), and precision, recall, and F-score at
\SI{5}{cm} and \SI{10}{cm}.

\paragraph{Analysis.}
The co-primary outcomes are PSNR and \SI{5}{cm} surface F-score.  We report all
eight active--random paired effects, means within trajectory, the grand mean,
sign counts, and a trajectory-cluster bootstrap interval.  The four seed-42
control sets are reported separately.  Decision rules and missing-run behavior
were committed before confirmatory outcome inspection.  The primary gate
requires positive grand-mean effects on both co-primary outcomes.  Replication
requires favorable effects on at least six of eight pairs and three of four
trajectory means for both outcomes.  The diagnostic gate requires positive
mean Spearman correlation and AUROC above 0.5 in at least six of eight seed
models.  We enumerate all $4^4=256$ trajectory-cluster resamples; with four
trajectory clusters, the resulting intervals are descriptive rather than
asymptotic inference.

All candidate scores are computed without candidate RGB or reference depth.
Reference depth is introduced only after the selection is frozen, to measure
diagnostic error ranking and final reconstruction quality.  Split manifests
are audited for exact 25/50-frame budgets, 10 validation and 20 test frames,
partition disjointness, retained seed membership, and identical holdouts across
methods.
