\begin{abstract}
We study offline frame acquisition for sparse-view 3D Gaussian neural maps.
Starting from a fixed set of posed observations, the method scores candidate
views with renderer-derived uncertainty and expands the map using a greedy
mixture of upper-tail uncertainty and camera-pose novelty.  We evaluate one
policy frozen before confirmatory training against matched random,
acquisition-time coverage, and pose-coverage controls on four independent
RGB-D trajectories.  The protocol uses disjoint temporal-block holdouts,
two optimization seeds for the primary comparison, and evaluates held-out RGB,
metric depth, and fused world-space surface geometry.  \todo{Replace this final

\end{abstract}
